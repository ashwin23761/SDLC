# KYC / AML Onboarding Standard (Sample, for prototype use only)

## 1. Customer Identification

**1.1 Customer identification** — Every applicant must provide full legal name, date of birth,
residential address, nationality, and a government-issued identification number before any
application can proceed.

**1.2 Document verification** — Identity documents must be verified against an authorised
identity-verification service before the application is marked as "identity confirmed."

## 2. Screening

**2.1 Sanctions screening** — Every applicant must be screened against current sanctions,
politically-exposed-persons (PEP), and adverse-media lists before approval. A potential match
must never be auto-cleared; it must be escalated to a compliance officer for manual review.

**2.2 Risk rating** — Each customer must be assigned a risk rating (Low / Medium / High) based
on factors including country of residence, occupation, product type, and transaction pattern.
High-risk customers require enhanced due diligence (EDD).

## 3. Ongoing Monitoring

**3.1 Periodic review** — Customer risk ratings must be reviewed at least annually (or every
6 months for High-risk customers).

**3.2 Record retention** — Onboarding records, screening results, and decisions must be
retained for a minimum of 5 years after the relationship ends.

## 4. Data Protection

**4.1 Minimum necessary data** — Only data necessary for identification, screening, and
regulatory reporting may be collected.

**4.2 Consent** — Explicit customer consent must be captured and logged before personal data is
used for any purpose beyond onboarding.
